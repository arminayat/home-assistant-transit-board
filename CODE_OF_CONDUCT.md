# Code of Conduct

Treat users, contributors and maintainers with respect. Discuss the work directly and in good faith. Technical disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption and sharing another person's private information are not.

Give actionable feedback, distinguish evidence from assumptions, accept reasonable corrections and respect decisions to keep the project small. Do not pressure others to provide unpaid work or immediate responses.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces. Maintainers follow the same standards. They may remove harmful content, limit a discussion or restrict participation, considering the context and seriousness of the behavior.

## Raising a concern

For a non-sensitive project concern, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/arminayat/home-assistant-transit-board/issues/new?title=Project%20concern%3A%20) with enough context to identify the problem. Keep personal details and harmful material out of the report.

For sensitive abuse, threats, harassment or exposed private information, use GitHub's private **Report abuse** action on the relevant content or account. See [GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). These reports go to GitHub under its policies; they are not private messages to this project's maintainer. This route also applies when a concern involves a maintainer.

Do not repost sensitive allegations or private information publicly. Project concerns are handled as maintainer availability permits; no particular response time or outcome is promised.
